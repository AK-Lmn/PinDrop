# 📌 PinDrop — Pinterest Video Downloader User Guide

PinDrop is a free, web-based tool designed to help you download high-quality videos from Pinterest instantly. It is free to use, requires no registration, and respects your privacy by processing everything statelessly (nothing is saved on our servers).

---

## 🚀 How to Download Pinterest Videos

Follow these simple steps to save any video from Pinterest to your device:

### 1. Copy the Pinterest Link
- **On desktop (Web)**: Open Pinterest, click on the Pin containing the video you want to download, and copy the URL from your browser's address bar.
- **On mobile (App)**: Open the Pinterest app, tap the **Share** button on the Pin, and select **Copy Link**.

### 2. Paste the Link on PinDrop
- Paste the copied Pinterest URL directly into the dark input field at the center of the PinDrop page.
- *Tip: PinDrop supports both standard Pinterest URLs and short links (`pin.it`).*

### 3. Fetch the Video
- Click the **Fetch Video** button.
- PinDrop will securely parse the page, find the highest-quality video stream (MP4 or HLS), and generate a preview for you.

### 4. Download and Save
- Once the preview card appears, click the **Download Video** button.
- The video will stream directly to your browser and trigger a "Save As" file download. Check your device's default **Downloads** folder.

## ⚡ Supported Media & Formats

- **Standard MP4 Videos**: Saved as standard video files compatible with all media players, smartphones, and web browsers.
- **HLS (.m3u8) Streams**: Saved as playlist stream files. For HLS streams, we recommend playing them with **VLC Media Player**.
- **pin.it Short Links**: PinDrop automatically resolves mobile share links instantly.

## 🛡️ Privacy & Safety

- **Stateless Downloads**: PinDrop does not store any files, histories, or user data. Once you close the tab, all session details are gone.
- **Direct Streaming**: Videos are fetched directly from Pinterest's official CDN networks to your browser, ensuring secure and fast downloads.
- **No Account Required**: No registrations, no logins, and no fees.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I download videos from private boards?
No. PinDrop can only fetch videos from public Pins. Private boards or protected pins cannot be accessed by our downloader.

### Why is my HLS download saving as a `.m3u8` playlist?
Some Pinterest videos are stored as adaptive HLS playlists. They can be played using modern media players like VLC or video editing tools that support network streams.

### Is there a limit to how many videos I can download?
No, there are no limits or caps. You can download as many Pinterest videos as you like.
